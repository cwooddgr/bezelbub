## What changed (user-visible, both apps)

- Three new devices, 48 in all: iPad mini (A20 Pro) in Midnight and Starlight, and MacBook Pro 14" and 16" with the PROLED XDR display in Silver and Space Black.
- A Mac capture from a 16" PROLED MacBook Pro at any of its five standard display zoom sizes (4128×2694, 3472×2266, 2994×1954, 2632×1718, 2332×1522) picks that model alone. A Bezelbub user ran our `Scripts/display-modes.js` on one to get the sizes.
- The 14" PROLED matches exactly only at its native 3024×1986 so far; other 14" sizes match by aspect ratio, so the user picks between MacBooks.
- Pricing is unchanged.

## Approved App Store text

• Added bezels for the new iPad mini (A20 Pro) in Midnight and Starlight, and for the 14" and 16" MacBook Pro with the PROLED XDR display in Silver and Space Black. You now have 48 devices to choose from.
